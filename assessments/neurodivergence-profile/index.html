Neurodivergence Trait Profile

A structured questionnaire mapping measurable ADHD, autism-spectrum, sensory, learning and motor traits onto the dimensions used in validated instruments (ASRS, Vanderbilt, SNAP-IV, BDEFS/BRIEF, AQ, RAADS-R, CAT-Q, TAS-20, Sensory Profile, ARHQ, DCDQ). Every dimension is measured by at least three questions. It produces a profile, not a diagnosis.

You are looking at example answers, not your own. They show how the profile reads. Clear them to start the questionnaire.
Clear every answer on this form? This can’t be undone.

This tool is for self-reflection and to structure a conversation with a qualified clinician. Items are paraphrased adaptations written around the constructs of the cited instruments (many of which are copyrighted); scores are not normed against a population sample and cannot be compared one-to-one with official cut-offs. A diagnosis of ADHD, autism, a specific learning disorder or DCD requires a clinical assessment, developmental history, evidence of impairment in more than one setting, and ruling out other explanations.
